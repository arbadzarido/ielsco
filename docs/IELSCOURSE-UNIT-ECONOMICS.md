## Findings and recommendations
1. **Collective is healthy.** It breaks even at 4 students and is strongly positive at the 8-student opening size (about 47% margin per student). Keep Rp199,000.
2. **Studio is healthy under every tested tutor rate** because the 4 sessions per month are shared by up to 5 students. Even at Rp150,000 per session it stays positive at 3 students. Keep Rp399,000. Standardise sessions at **75 minutes** (practical for 3-5 speakers) and confirm the tutor rate.
3. **One is the thin product.** It stays positive only if the mentor is paid below about Rp70,000 per session (Intensive) to Rp74,000 (Starter). At Rp75,000 per session all three packages lose money once the free Elco AI month is counted. The Rp90,000 single session leaves more room (Rp90,000 less fee Rp2,700 = Rp87,300 before the mentor).
4. **No price is changed here.** If the mentor rate is above about Rp65,000, the smallest sensible adjustment is to raise Intensive to Rp649,000 (rate break-even about Rp75,500) or to reduce the included Elco AI month to the actual marginal cost. Needs your decision with the real mentor rate.
5. Studio and Collective renewals depend on manual invoicing; no recurring card collection exists, so expect a renewal follow-up cost (staff time) not modelled.

## Costs that are unknown (explicit assumptions)
Mentor and Studio tutor rates, real Elco AI cost per active student (Rp25,000 assumed), payment fees for bank transfer (0% in practice; 3% kept as a conservative placeholder), Zoom/Meet licences, materials, marketing and acquisition cost, refunds, tax, admin time for verification and renewals.
